`IIndexingServiceAsync` observers follow the `IObserver` contract (#56). The `SearchDocument`, `DeleteDocumentRequest` and `DeleteAllDocumentsRequest` streams now behave the same way: `OnNext` is sent once after each operation the provider completed successfully, a rejected document or failed operation sends nothing (the returned `Result` carries the reason), `OnError` is never sent, and `OnCompleted` is sent once, when `IndexingServiceAsync` is disposed. Before, a rejected document sent `OnCompleted` while later documents still sent `OnNext`, `OnNext` for an index or delete was sent before the provider ran, even when it then failed, and deletes sent `OnCompleted` after every call. Observer callbacks are thread-safe, an observer that throws is logged through `ILogger` and skipped instead of failing the operation or skipping the other observers, `Dispose` never throws, subscribing after disposal completes the observer straight away, and a duplicate subscription stays active until each of its handles is disposed. `SearchServiceAsync` follows the same contract for its `ISearchModel` stream. Breaking: `IIndexingServiceAsync` and `ISearchServiceAsync` now extend `IDisposable`, so a custom service set through `SetIndexingServiceType<T>()` or `SetSearchServiceType<T>()` must implement `Dispose`; `IndexingServiceAsync` and `SearchServiceAsync` use the `protected virtual Dispose(bool)` pattern and take an optional `ILogger`.
