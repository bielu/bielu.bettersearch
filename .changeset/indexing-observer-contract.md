`IIndexingServiceAsync` observers follow the `IObserver` contract (#56). The `SearchDocument`, `DeleteDocumentRequest` and `DeleteAllDocumentsRequest` streams now behave the same way: `OnNext` is sent once after each operation the provider completed successfully, a rejected document or failed operation sends nothing (the returned `Result` carries the reason), `OnError` is never sent, and `OnCompleted` is sent once, when `IndexingServiceAsync` is disposed. Before, a rejected document sent `OnCompleted` while later documents still sent `OnNext`, `OnNext` for an index or delete was sent before the provider ran, even when it then failed, and deletes sent `OnCompleted` after every call. Subscribing and unsubscribing are thread-safe; observers are called one at a time, and never after `OnCompleted`, only while a service instance is not used concurrently, otherwise an observer must be thread-safe. An observer that throws is logged through `ILogger` and skipped instead of failing the operation or skipping the other observers, `Dispose` never throws, subscribing after disposal completes the observer straight away, and a duplicate subscription stays active until each of its handles is disposed. `SearchServiceAsync` follows the same contract for its `ISearchModel` stream. Breaking: `IIndexingServiceAsync` and `ISearchServiceAsync` now extend `IDisposable`, so a custom service set through `SetIndexingServiceType<T>()` or `SetSearchServiceType<T>()` must implement `Dispose`; `IndexingServiceAsync` and `SearchServiceAsync` use the `protected virtual Dispose(bool)` pattern and take an optional `ILogger`. The DI scope owns both services: don't dispose an injected service, dispose the handle `Subscribe` returned. `Unsubscriber<T>` is no longer used by BetterSearch and is marked `[Obsolete]`; it will be removed before the stable release.
