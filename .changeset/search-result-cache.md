Add an opt-in search result cache (#119). `ISearchResultCache`, in Abstractions, is the injection point: `Configurator.AddSearchCache<TCache>()` registers your own, `AddMemorySearchCache()` registers the built-in `MemorySearchResultCache` over `IMemoryCache` (single node only), and the new `Bielu.BetterSearch.Caching.FusionCache` package adds `AddFusionSearchCache()`, an adapter over `IFusionCache` that needs a backplane when the application runs on several nodes. When a cache is added, `AddBetterSearch` decorates every `ISearchProviderAsync` and `IIndexingProviderAsync` registration, keyed or not, so the search and indexing services get the cache without changing. The search decorator caches successful searches under a key built from the provider, `SearchCacheOptions.KeyPart` and the whole query, tagged with the index; failed and cancelled searches are not cached, and the search service's observers are notified on a cache hit too. The indexing decorator evicts an index's cached searches after every write to it, failed writes, index creation and index deletion included, and before the indexing service notifies its observers. `SearchCacheOptions` sets the duration (30 seconds by default), per-index `Enabled`, `Duration` and `Tags`, and a `Skip` delegate. Without a registered cache nothing changes. Core now references `Microsoft.Extensions.Caching.Memory` and `Microsoft.Extensions.Options`. See the new Caching page.
