Every Elasticsearch and OpenSearch indexing method now returns a failed `Result` with the exception as its cause when a request throws, for example with `ThrowExceptions()` on (#71). On Elasticsearch, `IndexDocumentAsync`, `IndexMultipleDocumentsAsync`, `RemoveAllDocumentsAsync` and `DeleteIndexAsync` used to throw it to the caller; on OpenSearch, every method but `RemoveDocumentsAsync`, `IndexExistsAsync` and `EnsureIndexExistsAsync` did, and those methods also threw when the client factory threw anything but an `ArgumentException`. On Lifti, every indexing method now returns a failed `Result` that keeps the exception as its cause when a custom `ILiftiIndexManager` or client factory throws, where `DeleteIndexAsync`, `IndexExistsAsync` and `CreateIndexAsync` threw, and `IndexDocumentAsync`, `IndexMultipleDocumentsAsync`, `EnsureIndexExistsAsync` and `RemoveAllDocumentsAsync` dropped the cause; `CreateIndexAsync` returns every error from the manager instead of only the first message. `OperationCanceledException` now propagates from Lifti's `IndexDocumentAsync`, `IndexMultipleDocumentsAsync` and `EnsureIndexExistsAsync`, and from Meilisearch's `IndexDocumentAsync` and `IndexMultipleDocumentsAsync` when the client factory throws it, instead of becoming a failed `Result`.
